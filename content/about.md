Hi, I'm Evan! I graduated in 2026 from Colorado School of Mines with an MS in data science, after graduating in 2025 with my BS in computer science, and have been working as a data scientist since then. However, during college I found a love for education, and have found an outlet for that in helping teach my friends and family about the most interesting or essential pieces of the tech world.

I started this site to further share that information that I was writing and researching anyway. There is an absurd amount of tech-related news constantly inundating feeds and our attention, but so many sources are so hungry for engagement that it's hard to tell if the information you are getting is useful or just a scare tactic. Everything is related to each other and builds on a foundation of information that you may not have, and I'm hoping I can help be a somewhat neutral third party to translate.

My goal here is ultimately to make it easy to follow trends, easy to learn about context that many articles you see won't give you, and easy to learn everything you may need to fully understand it.

Sometimes, I also just think something is cool and want to write about it.

Quick disclaimer that I do not have any relevant inside information about the industry and will try to keep things mostly objective, but my feelings and voice will obviously shine through occasionally. I will cite sources whenever I have them, especially for current events, but a lot of what I know and the context I can provide comes from my education or daily work. Also, I don't use AI for anything I write, but AI-written articles and results are hard to detect while doing research. I will do my best to verify the information I find, and I hope to be more trustworthy and consistent than those sites.

I am more than happy to answer any specific questions anyone reading this has, just email me at eteconomou@gmail.com and I'll either respond directly or make a post here depending on how broadly applicable your question is.
