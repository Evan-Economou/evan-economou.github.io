I'm a data scientist, and I've always been interested in education. A lot of my job is reading research, working with data, and interpreting what it means for people who don't do that all day. This site is my way of hopefully helping a non-technical audience to understand the world as it is today with all the complexities of AI news and issues.

I'm mostly planning to write about two kinds of things. The first is (hopefully) consise education materials around AI, tech, and cybersecurity topics I think are worth understanding, with a focus on non-technical language for the average person. The second is my perspective on news and current events, with a focus on taking out the profit-focused scare tactics we see everywhere on social media and being able to present an overarching image of what is important. There's a bit more about me on the [about page](/about/).
