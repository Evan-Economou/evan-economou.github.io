I'm a data scientist, and I've always been interested in education. A lot of my job is conducting and reading research, working with data, and interpreting what it means for people who don't do that all day. This site is my way of hopefully helping an audience that isn't constantly wrapped up in those topics to understand the essential pieces of the world as it is today with all the complexities of tech news and issues.

I'm mostly planning to write about two kinds of things. The first is education materials around AI, tech, and cybersecurity topics I think are worth everyone, even outside of the tech space, to understand. To achieve this, I'm going to try to focus on non-technical language and breaking up topics so it is easy to decide how deep you want to delve. The second is my perspective on news and current events, with a focus on taking out the profit-focused scare tactics we see everywhere on social media and being able to present an overarching image of what is important using the context I know from my work in the AI industry. There's a bit more about me on the [about page](/about/).
